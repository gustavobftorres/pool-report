# Balancer Metrics Report

This document provides a detailed explanation of the quantitative metrics used to analyze Balancer pools.
All formulas and definitions are strictly aligned with the implemented queries and observed data.

---

## 1. TVL (Total Value Locked) — `tvl_usd`

**Definition**  
Represents the total value, in USD, currently deposited in the pool by all liquidity providers.

**Formula**
TVL_day = ∑ pool_liquidity_usd

**Economic meaning**  
TVL measures the *stock of capital* available to absorb trades. A higher TVL generally implies a greater capacity to handle volume with lower price impact, but it does **not** imply efficiency or profitability by itself.

## 2. Volume — `volume_usd`

**Definition**  
Total USD value of all swaps executed in the pool during a given day.

**Formula**
Volume_day = ∑ amount_usd

## 3. Price Impact — `impact_p50_at_X`

**Definition**  
Median realized price deviation caused by trades of a given notional size (10k, 50k, 100k, 250k USD).

**Formulas**
P_exec = amount_usd / token_bought_amount
P_mid = P50(P_exec) (using trades ≤ 5k USD)
impact = |P_exec − P_mid| / P_mid

Copiar código

**Economic meaning**  
This metric measures *realized liquidity depth*, not theoretical slippage.  
Lower impact means deeper liquidity and better execution quality.

## 6. Effective Fee

**Definition**  
Observed, ex-post fee actually paid by traders.

**Formulas**
effective_fee_gross = fees_gross / volume
effective_fee_net_lp = fees_net_lp / volume

**Economic meaning**

## Summary

These metrics jointly describe:
- pool size (TVL)
- liquidity usage (Volume, Vol/TVL)
- execution quality (Impact, Elasticity)
- monetization (Fees, Effective Fee)
- returns (Fee APR)
- risk (IL Exposure)
- behavior and stability (Arbitrage Ratio, Stickiness)

They are designed to support quantitative pool comparison, risk-aware LP decisions, and machine-learning–based analysis.
